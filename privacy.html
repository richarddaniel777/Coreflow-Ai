CCoreFlow AI

PRIVACY

Your business data

CoreFlow AI stores your account details, business profile, leads, contact details, notes, interaction history, follow-up tasks, and AI-generated scores and drafts in Supabase.

When you request a score or draft, lead details and relevant business profile information are sent to the configured AI provider to generate that result. Review the provider’s privacy terms before using this feature with sensitive information.

CoreFlow AI does not send messages to customers automatically. You review and send any AI draft yourself.

To delete account data, ask the workspace administrator to delete your user in Supabase Authentication. The database is configured to cascade that user’s associated records. This product does not currently provide self-service account deletion.
